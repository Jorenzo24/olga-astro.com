About the astrologer

About me

Welcome to my site. My name is Olga Matyushkina and I am a professional astrologer.

My path into astrology began many, many years ago, back when hardly anyone knew what it was or what to make of it. It started with a general interest, then came formal professional training, which has never really stopped, and for more than 9 years now I have been giving consultations.

How I see astrology

Astrology is a discipline built on centuries of knowledge

Astrology is a discipline that holds centuries of knowledge, knowledge that explains the connection between a person and the world around them, and with the Universe. It helps you understand yourself, your path and the chains of cause and effect in this incarnation.

Through the natal chart we can look into a person's unconscious, see their mental world, understand themselves, their values and the tasks set before them, uncover and realise their talents, find harmony with themselves and become the true author of their own reality. Astrology is one of the paths to self-knowledge, and that is its most valuable practical use. It helps a person recognise the traits, the motives and the longings they are sometimes afraid to admit even to themselves.

We can learn who suits us, we can change our standard of living, our means, our health, improve the way our personal life unfolds, understand how to move through life's harder lessons more easily and, above all, discover the hidden possibilities worth developing so that life becomes fuller and brighter.
