Description of the service

The User is able to view all material available on the Site and to send requests for a consultation. Once a request has been sent, the Operator contacts the User (by email or telephone) and confirms the consultation booking.

Information about requests is not accessible to other users: confidentiality is fully respected.

Account creation

Using the Site requires no registration and no personal account. However, when sending a request, the User must provide contact details: name, email address and telephone number. These details are used only to arrange the booking and to select the consultation. Once that is done, the information is deleted.

The Operator reserves the right to restrict access to the service where the rules of fair use are breached, namely:

  • use of another person's or of invalid contact details;
  • use of the service for commercial, unlawful or self-serving purposes.

Processing of personal data and confidentiality

Personal data provided by Users through the online request form is used solely to arrange consultations with the Operator. The data is not passed to third parties and is not used for any other purpose.

Use of personal data

The Operator may use personal data obtained through the online request form to create or update contact information in its own database. Such data comprises:

  • first name and surname;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • type of consultation.

The site also collects and processes anonymised visitor data (including cookies) by means of web statistics services.

The Publisher does not process special categories of personal data relating to racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, or sex life.

Rights of access, erasure and rectification

In accordance with articles 39 et seq. of Act No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 on data protection and with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every person has:

  • the right of access to their data;
  • the right to rectification;
  • the right to erasure;
  • the right to restriction of processing;
  • the right to data portability;
  • the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Ireland: the Data Protection Commission).

To exercise these rights you may contact us at info@olga-astro.com. For the security of the procedure, confirmation of your identity may be required.

Data security

Data transmitted between the site and the user is protected by the HTTPS protocol. The Operator safeguards personal data and takes every possible measure to prevent access by unauthorised persons, including:

  • the use of strong passwords;
  • password-restricted access to calendars;
  • automatic closing of sessions when inactive.

Should inaccuracies in personal data be found, the User may update them by sending the Operator a notice at info@olga-astro.com marked "Update of personal data".

Retention and erasure of data

We keep your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, namely to arrange the consultation booking and to send notifications, in secure databases.

Data provided with a request (name, contact details, email) is kept until the service has been delivered. Reviews and comments left on the site are kept indefinitely, in order to preserve a record and to inform other users.

We undertake that your data will be erased once those periods have elapsed, unless the law provides otherwise.

Please note: this retention period does not apply to personal data connected with the consultation work itself, which is not stored on this site. That data is processed by the practitioner in her own system, under a separate privacy policy, and is unconnected to this site.

Cookies

Some features of this site depend on the use of cookies. If you give your consent, these cookies will be set so that certain features provided by third-party services can work.

This site does not use cookies for marketing or commercial purposes. Only technical cookies, necessary for the site to work correctly, and anonymised cookies for statistical analysis are used.

Your prior consent is required before cookies are set, other than those strictly necessary for the site to work. These cookies last 90 days; once that period has elapsed you will be asked to confirm your choice again.

Refusing cookies, or deleting them from your device, will not affect the quality of your experience on the site. You may also configure your browser so that cookies are stored on your device automatically or, conversely, blocked.

Liability

The Operator makes every effort to ensure the site's content is reliable, secure and up to date. However the company accepts no liability for possible errors, omissions or temporary unavailability of the site. The User is solely responsible for the use made of the information provided on the site.

Changes to these terms

The Operator reserves the right to change these terms of use at any time and without prior notice, in response to legal or technical developments or to changes in the company's activity. We recommend reviewing the terms regularly to stay informed.
