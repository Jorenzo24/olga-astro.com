Love Astrology Reading

This consultation is for anyone who is searching for a partner to marry.

Sometimes everything else in life falls into place, work, friends, a home, and only your love life seems to stand still. Meetings do not turn into relationships, relationships run the same course every time, and the years slip by in waiting. Your birth chart knows more about this than you would think: it shows both the reason the pattern repeats and the way out of it.

We look at the whole question of marriage and partnership in your chart: what stops you meeting your person, the kind of partner who suits you for a family, where the meeting is most likely to happen and what the chances of marriage are in the years ahead.

Relationship astrology

What a love astrology reading actually shows

In a birth chart, marriage has its own address: the seventh house and its ruler, the placement of Venus and Mars, the Moon as a picture of the person you look to for warmth. Those same points describe what you repeat without meaning to: the people you choose, what you expect from them, and the moment a relationship starts running its familiar course.

A love astrology reading reads exactly that part of the chart. It does not answer are we compatible, that is what a synastry reading is for, but the question that comes earlier: why there is no partner yet and what has to change for one to appear. That is why it is built on one chart, yours.

Most people come to this after a long stretch alone, or after several similar relationships that ended the same way. Astrology will not promise you a wedding. It will name the reason the pattern repeats, describe the kind of person marriage is genuinely possible with, and show the periods when a meeting is most likely.
